Applied machine learning / course project
01 — Sales data / regression

Not just a higher R².
A defensible result.

A Jupyter Notebook exploring a regression problem using sales data. The goal is to find a strong R² score while examining how meaningful and reliable the result is.

Rather than treating a metric as the whole story, the notebook explains why particular code and machine-learning approaches were chosen, making the reasoning behind the analysis part of the project.

Notebook link and measured results can be added after publication. No performance numbers are claimed here.
What the work emphasizes
  • ContextSales data and a regression objective
  • ToolsJupyter Notebook · machine learning
  • MethodExplained code choices and reasons for selecting ML algorithms
  • EvaluationR², with attention to the validity of the result
  • StatusIronhack mini-project · documentation available to add

About

I'm Andreas. My background combines customer-facing technical support, philosophy and logic, independent creative work and a growing focus on applied AI.

I am currently taking AI Engineering training at Ironhack, building Mnemo and preparing to complete my philosophy degree. I also have practical experience with memory techniques, especially the method of loci and story-based associations.

“Let AI work with you, not against you.”

I see AI as a complement to education: a tool that can support curiosity, understanding and independent thinking when used thoughtfully — rather than a substitute for learning.

I value work that has a clear purpose. I enjoy ambitious, creative projects that leave room for experimentation and end with something tangible, whether that is an analytical notebook, a learning app or a finished EP.

I speak four languages and am interested in applied AI, technical solutions and product-oriented roles.
